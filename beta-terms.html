Founder Beta Terms

Last updated: 4 November 2026

These terms cover the Chiket Founder plan offered to Android closed-beta testers. They are short on purpose. If something here is unclear, ask before you sign up rather than after.

1. What you get

If you join the closed beta and complete the test period below, your account becomes a Founder account:

  • Every feature in the Pro plan, at no charge.
  • No card is required to join, and none will be required later to keep it.
  • It stays free for as long as Chiket operates — see section 4 for what that means.

2. It is tied to one Google account

The Founder plan belongs to the single Google account you sign up with. It is not transferable, cannot be moved to another email address, and cannot be shared between people or households. If you create a second account, it is an ordinary account.

We need a Google account specifically because Android closed testing runs through Google Play, and Play can only issue test invitations to Google accounts.

3. What we ask of you

To keep the Founder plan:

  • Stay in the test for at least 14 days. Google requires testers to remain opted in continuously for that period; if you leave early the test does not count, for you or for us.
  • Use it like a real driver — add the vehicles you actually own and the block you actually park on. A dormant account tells us nothing.
  • Tell us what breaks. That is the entire point of a beta. Bugs, confusing screens, alerts that arrive wrong or not at all — we want to hear it.

If an account never opens the app during the test period, we may decline to grant the Founder plan. We will contact you before we do.

4. What “free for life” means

It means the lifetime of the service, not of the person. Plainly:

  • For as long as Chiket runs, your Founder account is not billed.
  • If Chiket shuts down, the plan ends with it. We cannot promise to run a service forever, and we would rather say so now than imply otherwise. Nothing was paid, so nothing is refundable.
  • If Chiket is sold or transferred, we will ask the new operator to honour Founder accounts, but we cannot bind them to it.
  • The set of features in “Pro” may change as the product develops. Founder accounts get whatever Pro includes at the time, not a frozen copy of today's feature list.
  • We may end a Founder account for abuse — sharing credentials, automated scraping, or using the service to break the law.

5. This is beta software

Do not rely on Chiket as your only reminder. During the beta, alerts may be delayed, may arrive wrong, or may not arrive at all. Notification delivery depends on your phone, your operating system and services outside our control.

You remain responsible for your own parking. Chiket does not pay your fines, cannot cancel a ticket, and is not liable for a ticket, boot or tow you receive while using it — including one an alert failed to warn you about.

Schedule and violation information comes from the City of Chicago. We pass it on as we receive it; we do not control its accuracy or timing.

6. Your data

What we collect and who sees it is set out in the Privacy Policy. You can delete your account at any time from inside the app — see the account deletion page. Deleting your account ends the Founder plan with it.

7. Changes

If we change these terms we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change materially reduces what a Founder account gets, we will tell existing Founders directly.

8. Contact

Questions about the beta or these terms: privacy@chiket.app.
