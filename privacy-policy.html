Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Chiket (“we”, “us”) helps drivers in Chicago avoid parking fines by watching street sweeping schedules, winter parking bans, permit expiry dates and unpaid tickets. This policy explains what personal data the Chiket app and service collect, why, who else sees it, and how you delete it. It applies to the Chiket mobile app and this website.

1. What we collect

We only collect what the app needs to do its job. You give us most of it directly.

What we do not collect

  • No location history. We never store where you have been. See Location data for exactly when the app uses your location.
  • No card details. Payments are handled entirely by Stripe on their own pages. Card numbers never reach our servers.
  • No photos, camera, contacts, calendar, microphone or files. The app does not ask for any of these.
  • No advertising identifiers — no IDFA, no Google Advertising ID, no device fingerprinting.

2. Location data

Chiket asks for your phone's location (precise and approximate) and uses it for two features only. Both are optional: the rest of the app works without location, and you can withdraw the permission at any time in your phone's settings.

Finding your street-sweeping zone (foreground)

When you tap to find your zone on the Zones screen, the app reads your current location once and sends those coordinates to our server, which looks up the Chicago ward and section you are in and returns it. The coordinates are used for that single lookup and are not saved — not in our database and not in our logs. What we keep is only the ward and section, if you then choose to watch it or mark it as where you parked. This happens only while the app is open and only when you tap.

Red light and speed camera alerts (background)

If you switch on Camera alerts, the app asks for location access “all the time” (Android) or “Always” (iPhone), so it can warn you while you drive with the screen off or the app in the background. While alerts are on:

  • Your phone downloads the public list of City of Chicago red light and speed cameras from us, and compares your position, heading and speed against that list on your phone.
  • Your location is never sent to our servers or to anyone else. The last few positions are held in memory only to work out your direction of travel.
  • To avoid repeating an alert, the app saves on your phone which camera it last warned you about and when. This stays on the device and never leaves it.
  • On Android a persistent notification shows while location is being used in the background; on iPhone the system location indicator shows.

Switching Camera alerts off stops location tracking immediately. Signing out also stops it and removes the downloaded camera list. Uninstalling the app removes everything it saved on your phone.

In short: we never store your location or location history, never share it, never sell it, and never use it for advertising. Location is used only to find your zone when you ask, and on your own phone for camera alerts you switch on.

3. Why we collect it

  • To run your account — sign you in, let you reset a password.
  • To check your vehicles — once a day we ask the City of Chicago whether the plates you added have new or escalating tickets.
  • To warn you in time — before street sweeping on your block, before a winter parking ban, before a permit expires, and before unpaid tickets reach the threshold at which Chicago boots a car.
  • To handle payment if you choose a paid plan.
  • To process an appeal you ask us to submit.
  • To use your location for the zone lookup and camera alerts described above.
  • To keep the app working — app activity and crash reports help us find and fix bugs.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or any purpose unrelated to the above.

4. Who else sees your data

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. It reaches these parties only because the service cannot work otherwise:

WhoWhat they receiveWhy
City of Chicago Licence plate, registration state and the surname on the registration This is the city's own ticket search. It is the only way to find out what a vehicle owes. We send it from our servers, not from your phone, and we do not send your email address, your account or any device identifier with it.
Stripe Your email address and an account reference, if you subscribe To take payment. Stripe handles the card itself under their own privacy policy.
Google (Firebase) and Apple Your device's push token and the text of an alert They are the delivery channel for push notifications on Android and iOS. Alert text can name a plate or a ticket amount.
Our email provider Your email address and the message To send password resets, and to deliver an appeal you submit.
Mapbox Only the address text you type when searching for a zone To turn an address into a location. We send it from our servers. No plate, account, device or location data is sent.

Your device location is not shared with any of these parties.

We also publish schedule and camera data drawn from the City of Chicago's public open-data portal. That is a one-way read of public information — nothing about you is sent to it.

We may disclose data if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or someone's safety.

Paying a ticket leaves our app. When you tap to pay a fine, your phone opens the City of Chicago's payment website in your own browser. The link carries the plate and surname so the city can find the ticket. From that point you are on the city's site, under their privacy policy, and they see your connection directly.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data. Ever, to anyone.
  • There is no third-party analytics, tracking or advertising software in the app. No Google Analytics, no Facebook SDK, no attribution or ad network, no third-party crash reporting. App activity and crash reports go only to our own servers.
  • We do not track your location or keep any record of where you have been.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We keep very little on your device. Your sign-in token is kept in your phone's secure storage. If camera alerts are on, the public camera list and the last camera you were warned about are saved too; uninstalling the app removes them.
  • We do not send marketing SMS. The app has no SMS capability at all.
  • We do not use social sign-in, so no social network is told you use Chiket.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your vehicles, zones and permits for as long as your account exists, because the service is a continuous watch — we cannot warn you about next month's sweeping if we forget your block this month. Some data is deleted sooner on its own:

  • Location coordinates — not kept at all.
  • “Parked here” spots — 14 days.
  • Crash reports — 30 days (summaries 90 days).
  • Notification history — 90 days.
  • App activity — 180 days.

When you delete your account, we delete the data associated with it. Two exceptions:

  • Payment records. If you ever paid, Stripe retains the transaction record and we retain proof of payment, because tax and accounting law requires it.
  • Anything the law requires us to keep, for as long as it requires.

7. Deleting your data or your account

Delete data, keep the account

You can remove a car, a watched block or a permit yourself in the app at any time, and switch off camera alerts to stop all use of location in the background. To have us delete some or all of the other data we hold about you without closing your account, use the data deletion request form or email privacy@chiket.app. We complete requests within 30 days.

Delete the account

You can delete your Chiket account yourself, from inside the app: open Profile, scroll to the bottom, and tap Delete account. It is permanent.

Full instructions are on the account deletion page.

If you have a paid subscription, cancel it before deleting the account, or billing may continue.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, object to how we use it, or receive a copy of it.

You can change your password, remove your cars, zones and permits, and delete your account in the app; you can ask us to delete your data while keeping the account. For anything else, email us at the address below and we will respond. We will not charge you or make it difficult.

9. Security

Passwords are stored as one-way hashes and are never readable by us or by anyone with access to our database. Card details never touch our systems. Access to production data is limited to the people who run the service.

No system is perfectly secure, and we will not pretend otherwise. If we discover a breach affecting your data, we will tell you.

10. Children

Chiket is for licensed drivers and vehicle owners. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will remove it.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your data, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Material changes will be announced in the app.

12. Contact

For any question about this policy, or to exercise any of the rights above, email privacy@chiket.app.
