Chicago snow watch

Chicago’s 2-inch snow ban covers about 500 miles of main streets. The City can activate it once 2 inches of snow is on the street, at any hour, and cars parked there can be ticketed or relocated. It is not activated often, but check the signs where you park. City of Chicago winter parking rules
