Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Chiket (“we”, “us”) helps drivers in Chicago avoid parking fines by watching street sweeping schedules, winter parking bans, permit expiry dates and unpaid tickets. This policy explains what personal data the Chiket app and service collect, why, who else sees it, and how you delete it. It applies to the Chiket mobile app and this website.

1. What we collect

We only collect what the app needs to do its job. You give us most of it directly.

DataWhat it is
Account Your email address, your last name, and your password. Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash — we cannot read them.
Vehicles For each car you add: the licence plate number, the registration state, and the last name printed on the registration. The city's ticket search requires all three.
Ticket information The parking tickets and fines the City of Chicago holds against the plates you add: ticket numbers, violation descriptions, dates, amounts due, notice level and hearing deadlines. This comes from the city, not from you.
Parking zones The ward and section you ask us to watch — effectively the block where you park. You pick this yourself in the app.
Permits The permit types you track and their expiry dates, so we can remind you.
Device A push notification token issued by your phone's operating system, so alerts can reach your device. It identifies the installation, not you personally.
Subscription Which plan you are on, and if you pay, an identifier from our payment provider that links your account to your subscription.
Support and appeals If you submit a ticket appeal, whatever you enter: your contact email, phone number and the tickets you selected.

Be aware of what this combination is. For a car you add, we hold the plate, the registered owner's surname, that vehicle's fine history, and the block you park on. Together that is sensitive. We treat it accordingly, and the sections below set out exactly where it goes.

What we do not collect

  • No device location. The app never reads your GPS or location. The parking zone is one you choose from a list or a map yourself.
  • No card details. Payments are handled entirely by Stripe on their own pages. Card numbers never reach our servers.
  • No photos, camera, contacts, calendar, microphone or files. The app does not ask for any of these.
  • No advertising identifiers — no IDFA, no Google Advertising ID, no device fingerprinting.

2. Why we collect it

  • To run your account — sign you in, let you reset a password.
  • To check your vehicles — once a day we ask the City of Chicago whether the plates you added have new or escalating tickets.
  • To warn you in time — before street sweeping on your block, before a winter parking ban, before a permit expires, and before unpaid tickets reach the threshold at which Chicago boots a car.
  • To handle payment if you choose a paid plan.
  • To process an appeal you ask us to submit.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or any purpose unrelated to the above.

3. Who else sees your data

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. It reaches these parties only because the service cannot work otherwise:

WhoWhat they receiveWhy
City of Chicago Licence plate, registration state and the surname on the registration This is the city's own ticket search. It is the only way to find out what a vehicle owes. We send it from our servers, not from your phone, and we do not send your email address, your account or any device identifier with it.
Stripe Your email address and an account reference, if you subscribe To take payment. Stripe handles the card itself under their own privacy policy.
Google (Firebase) and Apple Your device's push token and the text of an alert They are the delivery channel for push notifications on Android and iOS. Alert text can name a plate or a ticket amount.
Our email provider Your email address and the message To send password resets, and to deliver an appeal you submit.
Mapbox Only the address text you type when searching for a zone To turn an address into a location. No plate, account or device data is sent.

We also publish schedule data drawn from the City of Chicago's public open-data portal. That is a one-way read of public information — nothing about you is sent to it.

We may disclose data if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or someone's safety.

Paying a ticket leaves our app. When you tap to pay a fine, your phone opens the City of Chicago's payment website in your own browser. The link carries the plate and surname so the city can find the ticket. From that point you are on the city's site, under their privacy policy, and they see your connection directly.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data. Ever, to anyone.
  • There is no analytics, tracking or advertising software in the app. No Google Analytics, no Facebook SDK, no attribution or ad network, no crash reporting that sends your data anywhere.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not store your personal data on your device. The only thing saved locally is your sign-in token, kept in your phone's secure storage.
  • We do not send marketing SMS. The app has no SMS capability at all.
  • We do not use social sign-in, so no social network is told you use Chiket.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account exists, because the service is a continuous watch — we cannot warn you about next month's sweeping if we forget your block this month.

When you delete your account, we delete the data associated with it. Two exceptions:

  • Payment records. If you ever paid, Stripe retains the transaction record and we retain proof of payment, because tax and accounting law requires it.
  • Anything the law requires us to keep, for as long as it requires.

6. Deleting your account

You can delete your Chiket account yourself, from inside the app: open Profile, scroll to the bottom, and tap Delete account. It is permanent.

Full instructions are on the account deletion page.

If you have a paid subscription, cancel it before deleting the account, or billing may continue.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, object to how we use it, or receive a copy of it.

You can change your password and delete your account in the app. For anything else, email us at the address below and we will respond. We will not charge you or make it difficult.

8. Security

Passwords are stored as one-way hashes and are never readable by us or by anyone with access to our database. Card details never touch our systems. Access to production data is limited to the people who run the service.

No system is perfectly secure, and we will not pretend otherwise. If we discover a breach affecting your data, we will tell you.

9. Children

Chiket is for licensed drivers and vehicle owners. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will remove it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your data, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Material changes will be announced in the app.

11. Contact

For any question about this policy, or to exercise any of the rights above, email privacy@chiket.app.
