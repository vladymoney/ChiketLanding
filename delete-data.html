Delete your data

You can ask us to delete some or all of the data Chiket holds about you without deleting your Chiket account. Your sign-in stays, so you can keep using the app and start fresh.

If you want the account itself gone as well, use the account deletion page instead.

Request deletion

Use the email address your Chiket account is registered with. We reply from privacy@chiket.app to confirm the request came from you, then delete the data within 30 days and tell you when it is done.

What should we delete?

This opens your email app with the request filled in. Press send there to submit it.

Your email app should now be open with the request. Send it from the address on your account. If nothing opened, email privacy@chiket.app with your account email and what you want deleted.

Can't use the form? Email privacy@chiket.app from your account address and tell us which data to delete. That works just as well.

What you can delete yourself, right now

  • A car — open Cars and remove it. We stop checking its tickets from that moment.
  • A watched block — open Zones and remove it.
  • A permit — open Permits and delete it.
  • Location for camera alerts — turn Camera alerts off and location tracking stops. Your location is never sent to us or stored on our servers, so there is no location data of yours for us to delete.
  • Location permission — you can withdraw it at any time in your phone's settings.

What we have to keep

  • Your email address and password hash, because they are your account. Delete the account to remove them.
  • Payment records, if you ever paid. Tax and accounting law requires us and Stripe to keep proof of the transaction.
  • Subscription status, while you have an active plan, so your plan keeps working.

Full details are in our Privacy Policy.
