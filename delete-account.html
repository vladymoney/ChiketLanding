Delete your account

You delete your Chiket account from inside the app. Here is exactly where.

  1. Open Chiket and tap Profile in the bar at the bottom right.
  2. Scroll to the bottom, past Manage Subscription and Log out.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This is permanent. It deletes your account and the data stored with it, and it cannot be undone.
